RDM 0.3.16 applies speed-limit changes at once and no longer lets an automatic update interrupt a pending question or check. Browser extension: 0.4.12.

## Fixed

- Raising or removing a speed limit, global or per download, applies at once: connections no longer sleep on at the old rate (up to a minute at a few KiB/s).
- An automatic update waits while a browser download awaits your answer — it was lost, the browser having already handed it over — and while a finished file's checksum or VirusTotal analysis runs.

## Security

- Firefox: a download in a frame embedded by another site no longer receives that site's own cookies, which Firefox itself kept out of the frame (partitioned, or held back by SameSite).
- Safari: only your own clicks on links hand downloads over to RDM. A page's scripted clicks are left to Safari and its per-site download permission.

## Development

- The extension tests pass on a Windows checkout with CRLF line endings (`core.autocrlf`).

The audit scope, verification results and remaining limitations are documented in `docs/audit-2026-11-08.md`.
