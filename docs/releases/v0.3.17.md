RDM 0.3.17 gives each download from the browser a window of its own, like IDM. Browser extension: 0.4.12 (unchanged).

## New

- **Download windows**: a download from the browser — a file, or the video button — opens a window of its own, apart from the main one and in front of the others, even while RDM sits in the notification area only.
  - Before it starts: its name and folder (the category picks the folder; *Always use this folder* keeps a new one), and under *More options* its connections, speed limit, queue and checksum, for that download only. **Download**, **Later** (added paused) or **Cancel**; its size, and whether it can resume, are asked from the server meanwhile.
  - Then the same window shows its progress, the details folded, with Pause, Cancel, Hide and *Close when done*.
  - Once done: **Open**, **Open the location**, **Close**, its SHA-256 and a VirusTotal scan.
  - Several downloads cascade, five windows at once; double-clicking a running download in the list brings its window back.
- **Settings → Browser → Show the progress window**: off, the window closes once the download is accepted. With *Confirm downloads from the browser* off, the window opens straight on the progress.

## Changed

- A browser download whose file is already in the folder is asked about in its own window (*Keep both* or *Replace*). Pasted and copied links keep the prompt in the main window.

## Security

- `url` 2.5.9 (RUSTSEC-2026-0592: stack overflow in `Url::origin()` on nested `blob:` URLs).

## Known limitations

- On Wayland (Linux), the compositor decides where a new window goes and may keep it behind the others.
- Videos recorded in the browser while they play start from the page and get no window.
